Pint's Loot v1.0 - every piece of loot in Classic, a wishlist, and which of your characters own it
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Works on its own.
  Optional:  Pint's Alts           - adds the "Owned" column (which of your characters have the item, and where)
             Pint's Craft Upgrades - adds the "Upgrade" column (how much better it is than what you're wearing)

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsLoot
  Restart the game.

HOW TO USE
  /loot (or /ploot), or the minimap button: left-click to browse loot, right-click for your wishlist.

  LOOT - six sections, picked from the drop-downs at the top (or Prev. / Next):
  - Dungeons & Raids  33 instances, Ragefire Chasm to Naxxramas, every boss and its drop chances
  - Professions       Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring,
                      Mining, Cooking and First Aid - what each recipe makes and where it's learned
  - PvP               Alterac Valley, Arathi Basin, Warsong Gulch and Rank 1-14 rewards, with prices
  - World Drops       Azuregos, Lord Kazzak, the four Dragons of Nightmare and world epics
  - Factions          reputation rewards from Argent Dawn, Timbermaw, Thorium Brotherhood, Cenarion
                      Circle, Zandalar, Brood of Nozdormu, Hydraxian Waterlords and Bloodsail
  - Collections       Tier 1-3 and other sets, mounts, companions, tabards and legendaries
  The left column lists each page's bosses or groups - click one to see only that one.
  "Search all loot..." finds any item across every section (over 4,600 items).
  Tick "Gear I can use" to hide what your class can't wear or wield.

  Each item shows its type, required level (red if you're too low), where it comes from,
  the upgrade % and which of your characters own it.
  Shift-click to link an item in chat, Ctrl-click to try it on.

  WISHLIST
  - Click the star on any item (or Alt-click the item) to add it to your wishlist.
  - Each item shows the boss, with the dungeon on the line beneath.
  - The left column filters by slot (Head, Legs, Trinket...) with a count for each.
  - Tick "All my characters" to see the wishlists of every character on the realm.

  TOOLTIPS
  Every item tooltip in the game shows where the item comes from and which of your characters
  have it on their wishlist. Untick "Show sources in item tooltips" to turn this off.
