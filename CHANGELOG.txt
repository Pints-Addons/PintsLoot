Pint's Loot - Changelog

v1.1.4
- WoW Forever: the 9 new dungeons are in the list (Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site: Wetlands, City of
  Dalaran, The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold, Shaper's Terrace) with the boss loot
  found so far (from wowtbc.gg). The game doesn't publish these items yet, so each one fills in - icon, tooltip,
  upgrade %, wishlist - once the game has shown it to you (loot window, chat link or tooltip), and is remembered.

v1.1.3
- Share your wishlist with another player, like a whisper: "Share my wishlist..." on the Wishlist tab (or /loot share
  Name). With Pint's Loot they're asked to keep it, and it shows on their Wishlist tab (right-click its heading to
  remove it). Without Pint's Loot you're offered to whisper the items to them as item links.
- The window closes when a fight starts, so it never hides what's attacking you (/loot combat keeps it open), and the
  close button always works in combat (on WoW Forever it could be blocked).
- The wishlist is shared with Pint's Alts: its Character tab has a Wishlist button (any character's list), and stars in
  its gear picker add items. Changes show in both add-ons straight away.

v1.1.2
- WoW Forever: money shows with the gold, silver and copper icons (the game's own coin text showed bare digits).

v1.1.1
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.

v1.1
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Uses the modern item functions where the old ones are gone.

v1.0
- First public release.
- Loot browser: Dungeons & Raids (33), Professions, PvP, World Drops, Factions and Collections,
  over 4,600 items, with drop chances, recipe sources and prices.
- Search across all loot; "Gear I can use" filter; Shift-click to link, Ctrl-click to try on.
- Upgrade % (with Pint's Craft Upgrades) and which of your characters own each item (with Pint's Alts).
- Wishlist: star or Alt-click any item; shows boss and dungeon; filter by slot; all-characters view.
- Item tooltips show where an item comes from and who has it on a wishlist.
- Minimap button: left-click to browse, right-click for your wishlist.
