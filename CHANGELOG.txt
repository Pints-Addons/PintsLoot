Pint's Loot - Changelog

v1.1.9
- Hunters can use thrown weapons after all (a hunter trained Thrown in game): they're suggested and counted as
  usable for hunters again, as before the last version.

v1.1.8
- Hunters can't use thrown weapons (warriors and rogues only): thrown weapons are no longer suggested or counted as
  usable for hunters.
- WoW Forever: a realm reported blank at login no longer files wish lists and loot under no realm.

v1.1.7
- Gear made for other classes only ("Classes: Priest") counts as not usable for your class - WoW Forever's items too,
  read from the item itself when the data has no class list (with Pint's Craft Upgrades).

v1.1.6
- WoW Forever: PvP is Forever's - what the PvP vendors sell there (Wowhead's WoW Forever NPC pages): a new Honor
  Quartermasters page (the armor, accessories, weapons and mount quartermasters in the capitals, Alliance and Horde),
  Forever's prices on the battleground and rank pages (gold, or honor plus battleground marks), and what Forever added
  to Warsong Gulch and Arathi Basin. PvP items no Forever vendor sells move to "Not confirmed on Forever".
- WoW Forever: every dungeon and raid boss's loot now comes from Wowhead's WoW Forever database (the same tables as
  Pint's Dungeon Journal), with real drop chances - the old dungeons no longer show Classic loot, and the new dungeons
  show real items instead of stand-ins. Classic Era keeps the Classic tables.
- WoW Forever: Professions lists every recipe Forever has, for all nine professions (2,176), by the skill it's learned
  at, with how it's learned - the same data as Pint's Professions and Recipe Sources.
- WoW Forever: Collections lists Forever's mounts, companions, tabards and legendaries, and a new WoW Forever Sets page
  (Rotmender's Raiment, Violet Sorcerer's Vestments, Krol'dok Battlegear and the rest).
- WoW Forever: Factions adds a page for each reputation Forever added (Durotar Supply and Logistics, Azeroth Commerce
  Authority, The Watchers, Kirin Tor, Earthen Ring, Nightclaw Druids, Theramore Expeditionary Force, Darkspear Raiders,
  Guardians of Hyjal, the Steamwheedle towns...) and Forever's new rewards on the Classic ones; PvP adds Forever's
  Arathi Basin rewards. From Wowhead's WoW Forever faction pages.
- WoW Forever: the last 51 items the new dungeons showed by name only (Blackmaw Hold's bosses, the Alcaz Prison and
  Drowned City quest rewards) are the real items now, found on Wowhead's WoW Forever database.
- WoW Forever: what only the Classic data has - raids that haven't opened, the world bosses' Classic tables, the Classic PvP
  lists, reputation rewards and sets Wowhead's WoW Forever pages don't show - waits out of sight in a "Not confirmed on
  Forever" category. "Show Classic-only data" at the bottom of the category menu brings it into view.

v1.1.5
- WoW Forever: fuller loot tables for all the new dungeons - every boss's known drops, rares and hidden bosses
  marked, a Quest rewards group for each dungeon, and new bosses: War Handler Ashka (Krol'dok), Captain Dreadrise,
  Primeval Elemental, Var'Taka, Deathless Marrow, Min'loth and Umbra (Drowned City), Zarla Ober (Alcaz Prison) and the
  8 bosses of Blackmaw Hold. Shaper's Terrace has no loot found yet.
- Items whose numbers are published (Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site, City of Dalaran, Krol'dok)
  now start as the real item, so their icon, type, required level and tooltip show straight away instead of a
  question mark. The rest still fill in once the game shows them to you.

v1.1.4
- WoW Forever: the 9 new dungeons are in the list (Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site: Wetlands, City of
  Dalaran, The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold, Shaper's Terrace) with the boss loot
  found so far (from wowtbc.gg). The game doesn't publish these items yet, so each one fills in - icon, tooltip,
  upgrade %, wishlist - once the game has shown it to you (loot window, chat link or tooltip), and is remembered.

v1.1.3
- Share your wishlist with another player, like a whisper: "Share my wishlist..." on the Wishlist tab (or /loot share
  Name). With Pint's Loot they're asked to keep it, and it shows on their Wishlist tab (right-click its heading to
  remove it). Without Pint's Loot you're offered to whisper the items to them as item links.
- The window closes when a fight starts, so it never hides what's attacking you (/loot combat keeps it open), and the
  close button always works in combat (on WoW Forever it could be blocked).
- The wishlist is shared with Pint's Alts: its Character tab has a Wishlist button (any character's list), and stars in
  its gear picker add items. Changes show in both add-ons straight away.

v1.1.2
- WoW Forever: money shows with the gold, silver and copper icons (the game's own coin text showed bare digits).

v1.1.1
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.

v1.1
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Uses the modern item functions where the old ones are gone.

v1.0
- First public release.
- Loot browser: Dungeons & Raids (33), Professions, PvP, World Drops, Factions and Collections,
  over 4,600 items, with drop chances, recipe sources and prices.
- Search across all loot; "Gear I can use" filter; Shift-click to link, Ctrl-click to try on.
- Upgrade % (with Pint's Craft Upgrades) and which of your characters own each item (with Pint's Alts).
- Wishlist: star or Alt-click any item; shows boss and dungeon; filter by slot; all-characters view.
- Item tooltips show where an item comes from and who has it on a wishlist.
- Minimap button: left-click to browse, right-click for your wishlist.
